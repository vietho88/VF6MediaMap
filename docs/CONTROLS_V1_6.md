# Controls (v1.6)

The left rail auto-hides after about 2.8 seconds. It is revealed **only** by tapping the
small `›` handle on the left edge; taps on YouTube/Web/VietMap do not reveal it.

Controls include media actions, VM show/hide, `VM E` edit, and the new VM preset control:

- `VM C`: Compact preset.
- `VM X`: Expanded preset.
- Tap `VM C/VM X` to toggle the current Phone/Car preset.
- Long-press it to reset the current surface layout.
- `VM E`: unlock overlay, one-finger drag and two-finger pinch resize.
- `VM ✓`: save/lock the current surface layout.
